# GetVocal

> GetVocal builds AI voice and chat agents for regulated, high-volume customer operations. Agents resolve customer conversations end to end while staying inside your business rules — with human-in-the-loop approval on high-stakes actions (large transactions, high-value returns, sensitive data). They run on a living model of your policies, are governed in real time, and improve from every human correction.

GetVocal serves enterprise customer-service and operations teams in telecom, financial services, e-commerce/retail, BPO, healthcare and hospitality. Agents handle voice and chat across your existing stack (CRM, CCaaS, telephony, helpdesk) and escalate to a human with full context whenever a conversation needs it. Deployment is flexible: on-premises, self-hosted, EU-hosted or hybrid.

## Product
- [Agent Builder](https://www.getvocal.ai/agent-builder): Build, debug and tune production-ready, multilingual agents from your SOPs, transcripts and policies — no engineering.
- [Control Tower](https://www.getvocal.ai/control-tower): Air-traffic control for your AI fleet — monitor live conversations, enforce policies, override any agent, and audit every decision.
- [Hybrid Workplace Platform](https://www.getvocal.ai/hybrid-workplace-platform): How human agents and AI agents work together, with the AI handling volume and humans handling judgement.
